Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

ClippedLive is a personal publishing tool. It is not a product offered to other people, it has no users other than its owner, and it has no sign-up.

This website

This site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics and no advertising or tracking scripts, and collects nothing about visitors. The pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, whose own server logs are governed by GitHub's privacy statement.

The application

The ClippedLive application runs on a single private computer. It does not have accounts, does not accept sign-ins from anyone, and does not receive data from members of the public.

It holds:

  • Access tokens for the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, used only to upload the owner's own videos to those accounts.
  • Recordings of public livestreams, and clips made from them, stored locally.
  • The public name and channel link of the creator each clip came from, so the clip can credit them.

Tokens are stored on that computer only. They are never sold, shared or sent anywhere other than the platform that issued them.

What it does not do

  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.
  • No advertising or marketing profiles are built.
  • No personal information is gathered about viewers, followers or commenters.
  • No data from the platform APIs is used for anything other than publishing the owner's own clips and reading back their public view counts.

Google user data

ClippedLive's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The only Google data it touches is the owner's own YouTube channel, for uploading the owner's own videos and reading the resulting public statistics. That data is never transferred to anyone, never used for advertising, and never read by a human other than the owner.

Retention

Source recordings are deleted automatically within days of being processed. Published clips and their view counts are kept while the accounts exist. Access tokens are deleted when revoked or when the tool is uninstalled.

Creators

If you are a creator whose stream appears in a clip and you want it removed, email clippedlivehq456@gmail.com. The clip will be deleted from every platform it was posted to, and your channel will be removed from the recording list.
