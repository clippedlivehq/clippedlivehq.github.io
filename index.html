ClippedLive

Short gaming clips

ClippedLive is a personal tool that turns moments from public gaming livestreams into short vertical videos and publishes them to the ClippedLive accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram.

What it does

  • Watches a list of public livestreams and records them.
  • Finds moments worth watching and edits them into short vertical clips.
  • Adds captions and a visible credit to the original creator on every clip.
  • Publishes approved clips to the ClippedLive accounts, after a human review.

Credit and removal

Every clip names the creator it came from. If you are a creator and want a clip taken down, email clippedlivehq456@gmail.com and it will be removed from every platform, and that channel will stop being recorded.
